Privacy.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information justaskmajor collects when you visit the site or contact me, who else handles it, and what choices you have. justaskmajor LLC is a one-person consultancy in Richardson, Texas, and I’m the person who reads your messages.

The short version

I don’t sell your information. I collect only what’s needed to answer your questions, understand how the site is used, and keep it running. A few outside services help with that, and they’re listed below so there are no surprises.

What you send me

If you fill out the contact form, you give me your name, email address, business or organization, what you’re interested in, and your message. The form is processed by Formspree, a form-handling service, which passes your message to me by email. I use it to reply to you and to scope any work we discuss. I keep it for as long as it’s useful for that relationship, and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

If you email me directly at hello@justaskmajor.com, I get whatever you include in that email.

Please don’t send passwords, financial account numbers, or other highly sensitive information through the form or by email.

What’s collected automatically

Microsoft Clarity. The site uses Microsoft Clarity, a free analytics tool, to understand how visitors use pages: where people click, how far they scroll, and where they get stuck. Clarity can record anonymized sessions and may set cookies in your browser. By default it masks text typed into form fields. Microsoft processes this data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement and the Clarity terms. I use the results only to improve the site.

Hosting. The site is served through Cloudflare, which handles requests to the site and may keep standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and performance. Cloudflare also hides email addresses on the site from automated scrapers.

Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google’s servers when a page loads.

Affiliate links

Some pages, including the Resources page and some blog posts, contain affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, justaskmajor.com earns from qualifying purchases. When you click an Amazon link, Amazon may set its own cookies and collect information under the Amazon Privacy Notice. I don’t receive your personal information from Amazon, only reports of qualifying purchases.

Purchases

Some products are sold through third-party platforms such as Gumroad. When you buy there, the platform collects and processes your payment and contact details under its own privacy policy. I don’t see or store your card number.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a content blocker to stop analytics scripts. The site will still work. You can email me to ask what I have about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, and I’ll respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is meant for adults running businesses and households. It isn’t directed to children, and I don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If I change this page, I’ll update the date at the top. If I start using a new kind of tool that handles visitor information, I’ll add it here first.

Contact

Questions about this page or your information: hello@justaskmajor.com, or use the contact form.
